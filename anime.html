My Top Five Anime

Anime I have been watching for over a decade now.

View the gallery
#1

Code Geass

My favorite anime of all time. Lelouch is one of the best protagonists I have seen. I also love how much strategy goes into this anime, for a while I would watch this anime once every 8 months or so, I think I have about 10 rewatches.

#2

JoJo's Bizarre Adventure

My friend first put me on when Part 4 had just came out for the anime. I loved it and I read the manga up to Part 8. There is so much that happens inside this anime and it is such a premium experience being able to watch this.

#3

Death Note

I love strategy and Death Note is that. Light and L doing mind games every episode for the first half was amazing, it felt like I just took a backseat not knowing what would be next.

#4

Persona 4 the Animation

I love the Persona series as a whole, and Persona 4 is my favorite out of all the games, but Persona 4 anime is just so great in the fact that they first choose all the hilarious options in game which gives Yu such a great personality, they beautfully adopt the plot from the game.

#5

Mirai Nikki

I love death games, and I love animes with someone who is unhinged. Luckily everyone in this anime is unhinged. This anime has sparked so many memes online, and I will say it is a little embarassing putting this one on the website. But I watched this to the full with my roommates and I also watched it prior when I was in middle school (which is honestly too young to watch this) really I would only have adults watch this. Also it deals heavily with Sci-fi and gods and it has very cool powers about the future.

Why I Like Anime

Anime is what I grew up on. What I got bullied for when I was younger. Now everyone likes anime with how modern animes are but this is something I have defended when I was younger. It holds such a heavy bond with me because every anime deals with different unique topics and it is so easy to get animes off of niche things like being killed and turned into a vending machine which is somehow going strong?? Anime will always be a part of my life.
