My Favorite Video Games

Explore the games

Dead by Daylight

I personally love Dead by Daylight because of how much skill goes into the game. It is a very flexible game where you have to be smart about your actions and cooperate with teammates.

Hitman

I love Hitman due to the fact that it is a total sandbox game. There are hundreds of ways to get your target in game and so many side stories within the game that it feels like you get just total freedom.

Portal 2

I love Portal, especially Portal 2 mostly because of the amazing work the team put into the game. The voice acting is phenomenal, the puzzles are very easily replayable as long as you give yourself a long enough break to forget the actual puzzles and the community made levels are just awesome.

Why I Enjoy Video Games

Video games are the greatest destresser ever. If I ever have a terrible day with work or school I could just go home and just play some games for a couple of hours and just feel right at home.

Another great thing about games is you can play it with friends. Besides the games on my list here I love being able to play games with my friends and we often buy each other games so we could just play with each other.
