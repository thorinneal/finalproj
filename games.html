My Favorite Video Games

There are more but these are my top picks!

Explore the games

Dead by Daylight

I love Dead by Daylight because of how much skill and decision-making go into every match. It is flexible, tense, and rewards smart teamwork.

Hitman

Hitman is a total sandbox. There are hundreds of ways to approach a target, and the side stories make every location feel full of possibilities.

Portal 2

The voice acting is phenomenal, the puzzles are replayable after enough time away, and the community-made levels give the game almost endless life.

Why I Enjoy Video Games

Video games are one of the greatest ways to destress. After a difficult day with work or school, I can play for a couple of hours and feel right at home.

They are also a great way to spend time with friends. We often buy each other games so we can experience them together.
