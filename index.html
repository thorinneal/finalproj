Hi, my name is Thorin. Over the course of this class I have made websites talking about my favorite music, my favorite video games and my favorite anime. I figured I would combine all of that into a final project where it is all in the same website.

My Favorite Songs of All Time

Boys Don't Cry

The Cure

This is by far one of my most favorite songs ever. You can just keep this song on loop and it's perfect BUT I will say the 1979 song is VERY much better than the 1986 remix that they keep trying to push out. I love obviously the main line "Hiding the tears in my eyes, 'cause boys don't cry" but SPECIFICALLY the first time he says it because he says it so mechanically but with so much passion that whenever I listen to it I always get into my feelings.

Eyes Without a Face

Billy Idol

I love the hypnotic theme of this song, it very HEAVILY contrasts like most of the songs in Rebel Yell. I own this album on Vinyl and whenever I play it I always love when it reaches this song. I love the french in the background saying "Les yeux sans visage" and the bit where he goes off on a tangent "When you hear the music, you make a dip into someones elses pocket then make a slip steal a car go to las vegas"

Otherside

Red Hot Chili Peppers

This song is also pretty unique from the previous album content. The heavy themes about drugs and their friend and late bandmate is excellently portrayed in this song. The music is so unique from literally anything they have made and I could listen to this on loop for days. Favorite line from this one is "She wanna know am I still a slut I gotta take it on the other side"

What I Am Listening to Right Now

Girls Just Want to Have Fun

Cyndi Lauper

I have just been BINGING this song I don't know why. I used to listen to it a lot when I was younger but something about it now is just tickling my brain in the sort of way that I can't describe. For example I was biking back from work today the sun was setting I was stopped at a traffic light, golden hour was happening with the sun and the line "I wanna be the one to walk in the sun" and I literally felt like this image I mean it was amazing.

Dancing with Myself

Generation X

I originally found this song when I was younger looking more into the discography of Billy Idol. I instantly loved his remixes of the song (since he is the lead singer of Gen X) especially the uptown mix, but recently I was listening to the original track and it's just so amazing to bike to. This also sounds strange since it IS a rock song but for a bit I have been taking naps in the middle of the day and I just leave this blasting in my headphones while I sleep and somehow I sleep comfortably. Favorite line by far is "But your empty eyes seem to pass me by leave me dancing with myself"

Around the World

A Touch of Class

It wouldn't be right NOT to have a Eurodance song on here. I have also been binging this song nonstop, the song takes me back to playing resident evil for some reason and I just love it. My favorite line from it is "And now the night is gone still it goes on and on so deep inside of me"
